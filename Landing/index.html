What is SNUSDEX?

SNUSDEX is a mobile application for iOS that acts as a comprehensive reference database for snus products. It allows users to scan snus product barcodes, view detailed product information, submit personal ratings, and build a personalised collection log. The app serves both casual users exploring new products and dedicated enthusiasts building complete brand catalogues.

How does the scanner work?

The SNUSDEX scanner uses the device camera to read EAN/UPC barcodes on snus cans. When a product is recognised, the app immediately displays the full product entry: brand, product name, nicotine format (loose/portion/slim/mini), nicotine strength in mg/g, flavour description, moisture content, region of origin, and its rarity tier classification. Unrecognised products can be submitted for addition to the database.

Does SNUSDEX track nicotine consumption?

Yes. SNUSDEX includes a nicotine intake tracking feature that calculates total nicotine consumed based on actual product mg/g data and the user's logged usage. The app displays daily average pouches, average daily nicotine intake in milligrams, and a cumulative lifetime total. This feature is designed for transparency and informed decision-making, not for restriction. All tracking data is stored locally on the device.

What is MouTrack?

MouTrack is a harm reduction feature within SNUSDEX that monitors placement-side patterns and recommends rotating which side of the mouth the user places snus on. Consistently placing snus on the same side of the mouth without rotation can cause localised gum recession over time. MouTrack analyses usage history and provides a simple, non-intrusive suggestion to switch sides when the active side has been used for a prolonged period, allowing gum tissue to recover.

What is the rarity tier system?

Every snus product in SNUSDEX is assigned one of six rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic. The tier is determined by a combination of factors including product availability, regional distribution, scan frequency across the user base, and historical market data. Rarity tiers are displayed prominently on each product entry and filter in the Dex catalogue.

What is the Collector Card?

The Collector Card is a personalised identity card generated for each SNUSDEX user. It displays the user's chosen username, total Dex score (derived from scan activity and rating quality), the number of unique products scanned, and their declared favourite brand. The card has a metallic finish and is customisable with different colour themes, background patterns, and animations. It can be shared directly from the profile screen.

Who is SNUSDEX for?

SNUSDEX is designed for adult snus users aged 18 and above. The primary audience is snus enthusiasts in Scandinavia, the United Kingdom, and other markets where snus is legally sold. The app is also used by retailers and brand representatives to track product perception and community ratings. A business-tier API is available for manufacturers and distributors seeking aggregated market intelligence.

Is SNUSDEX free to use?

Yes. SNUSDEX is free to download and use for individual users. All core features — scanning, rating, the Dex catalogue, social features, and the collector card — are included at no cost. A business-tier subscription is available separately for companies requiring access to aggregated analytics, regional market data, and brand-level sentiment reporting.
